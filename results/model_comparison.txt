Model comparison on the usual walk: 165 participants (93 PD, 72 controls), one recording each, identical splits for every model (checked fold by fold).
Within protocol: 5-fold participant-grouped cross-validation repeated 20 times; mean over the 100 test folds, 95% interval corrected for the overlap between training sets (Nadeau and Bengio 2003). Across protocols: each sub-study held out in turn, the model trained on the other two; AUC per held-out study and for all out-of-study predictions pooled, 95% participant-bootstrap intervals (2000 resamples). Drop: pooled AUC of the out-of-fold predictions (averaged over the 20 repeats) minus pooled out-of-study AUC, paired bootstrap.
